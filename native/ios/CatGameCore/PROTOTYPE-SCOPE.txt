CatGameCore — native prototype rules and persistence

This Foundation-only Swift package contains no UI, SpriteKit, networking, database,
login, browser-storage access, or cloud-save code. It is independent of the web game.

Implemented rules:
- Basic food costs 20 gold. Feeding uses 1 serving, or 2 for a pregnant cat,
  restores 25 satiety and 4 mood, capped at 100.
- Cleaning consumes 1 litter, restores 30 cleanliness and 4 health.
- Play requires 1 toy use. A 35-gold wand supplies 6 uses. Playing changes mood
  by 18 + floor(comfort / 20), intimacy by 12, energy by -10, player mood by +4.
- Five careers use the web catalog's base values:
  Job                    Level   Real minutes   Stamina   Mood   Gold   EXP
  Flyer Handout          1       2              10        5      60     22
  Convenience Store      2       3              15        8      100    35
  Pet Shop Assistant     4       4              20        12     160    52
  IT Engineer            6       10             30        12     400    100
  Cat Cafe Manager       10      30             50        20     800    200
  The cafe uses durationMinutes: 30, not the web's separate duration: 12 field.
  Locked careers and insufficient stamina cannot start or charge resources.
  Mood costs clamp at zero. Only one work shift can be active.
- Work completion grants its captured gold and EXP once. Each next level costs
  current level * 100 EXP; overflow EXP is retained and can cross several levels.
  Level thresholds unlock careers at levels 1, 2, 4, 6 and 10. The catalog does
  not impose an artificial gameplay level cap; numeric overflow is rejected.
  Web random events, random item rewards, player hunger, mood-dependent duration
  or fine adjustments, tasks, and bank repayment are not yet ported.
- player.lastWorkResult is the most recent already-paid receipt, not an unclaimed
  reward. It stores work ID, job ID, original deadline as completedAt, gold/EXP,
  levelBefore, levelAfter and expAfter. Newly unlocked careers are derived from
  the level range. Viewing a receipt does not pay again. Starting another shift
  keeps the receipt; the next completion replaces it.
- Six cosmetic avatars provide male and female variants of Sunny, Forest and
  Bookish looks. Avatar and gender choices never change work ability or rewards.
- Four starter equipment items are owned for free but initially unequipped:
  cap/head reduces work mood cost by 20%; jacket/body reduces stamina cost by
  15%; sneakers/feet reduce duration by 10%; charm/accessory reduces duration
  by 5%. The full set reduces duration/stamina/mood costs by 15%/15%/20%.
  Same-category reductions add and cap at 50%. Positive costs round up with a
  minimum of one; zero base costs remain zero. Durations round up to whole
  seconds. Gold and EXP rewards are unchanged. Fatigue relief is represented
  by reduced stamina expenditure, not by a separate fatigue statistic.
- GameEngine.workQuote supplies both the preview and the contract captured in
  activeWork.quote. Equipping, removing or changing appearance does not alter an
  active shift's deadline, costs or rewards; changes apply to the next start.
  The captured baseline is not repriced from a later catalog. Slots permit one
  owned item each, and removing equipment retains ownership. The starter gift
  IDs are explicit, so future purchasable catalog entries are not auto-granted.
- Player sleep recovers 20 stamina and 20 mood per real hour, capped at 100.
  Each stat earns 1 point per 180 seconds; its remaining seconds persist across
  synchronization, separate sleep sessions, and reloads. Capped stats do not bank
  extra recovery. Sleep continues until explicit wake-up, including while offline.
  Sleep and work are mutually exclusive; browsing and cat care remain available.
  Repeated starts/wakes and clock rollback cannot duplicate elapsed-time rewards.
- A cat may rest on an owned, placed catBed or floorCushion. The caller starts
  its session only after the cat has physically settled on the furniture.
  Bed rests last 12 seconds; cushion rests last 9. Energy is the only recovered
  cat stat: the session budget is 20 + floor(comfort / 20), capped at 100 energy.
  Earned energy is floor(budget * elapsed / duration), granted incrementally.
  Early exit settles only elapsed time. Successful care and changed room layouts
  end rest; failed care and unchanged layouts do not. Matching session IDs keep
  duplicate starts and late exit events from awarding again or ending a new rest.
- Cat satiety, cleanliness, mood, energy lose 1 per 12/8/6/5 elapsed minutes.
  Player stamina recovers 5 per elapsed awake hour, without stacking during sleep.
  Cat energy decay pauses during rest, preserving the pre-rest partial interval;
  other cat stats continue decaying. Rest expires automatically, grants at most
  its one-session budget offline, and resumes normal energy decay after expiry.
  Partial intervals are preserved.
  Disease, mortality, breeding progression, and health decay are deferred.

GameEngine actions are synchronous value mutations. synchronize must be called
on load/foreground and active ticks. Failed actions do not consume resources.
Care feedback follows successful state mutations. A furniture-arrival callback
may request startCatRest, but elapsed timestamps in the core determine rewards;
animation frames never award energy. On cold load, the app synchronizes first,
then ends any unfinished cat rest so a fresh scene starts safely on the floor.
Player sleep remains active across cold loads until explicit wake-up.
The Store should serialize snapshot saves in creation order and throttle passive
sync writes; the repository serializes disk operations but does not choose which
of two externally submitted snapshots represents the user's latest intent.
Gold, EXP, level advancement and the receipt belong to the same atomic work
completion snapshot. The Store publishes that snapshot and its result notice
only after successful saving. A failed save retains the uncompleted shift for
retry. Offline completion and clock rollback cannot pay the same shift twice.

Native saves:
- FileSaveRepository writes cat-life-native-v1.json in its caller-provided directory.
- A changed valid primary is retained as cat-life-native-v1.backup.json before
  replacing the primary with an atomic Data write.
- Invalid primary files can recover from a validated backup. The corrupt bytes
  are preserved in a separate unreadable-save-UUID.json file. The caller can
  inspect lastLoadDisposition to notify the player of backup recovery.
- When no usable backup exists, load throws rather than creating a new game.
- A future schema is refused and never replaced with an older backup.
- The model retains every native cats entry, including generated IDs.
- Schema remains 1. Player sleep, per-stat recovery remainders, and cat rest
  sessions live in the same snapshot. Legacy saves missing the new fields default
  to no active sleep/rest and zero remainders, retaining cats, resources, furniture,
  room layout, work, and settings. No separate persistence key is introduced.
- Career fields are player.level, player.exp, player.lastWorkResult, and
  activeWork.expReward in the same snapshot. Missing level/EXP fields default to
  level 1 and zero EXP; old completedWorkCount does not grant retrospective gold
  or EXP. Existing cats, supplies, furniture, layout, settings, sleep and work
  remain intact. Old in-flight work retains its ID, deadline and captured gold;
  missing EXP uses the known job's base value (22 for flyers), or zero for an
  unknown legacy job. Explicit saved EXP rewards are preserved. Invalid progress
  or receipt fields cannot overwrite a valid primary save or backup.
- player.profile contains avatarID, ownedEquipmentIDs and equipped slot IDs in
  the same schema-1 save. Only an entirely absent profile migrates to the four
  free starter items. An explicit empty inventory remains empty across reloads.
  Malformed or incomplete profile data is rejected, not treated as another gift.
  Unknown avatars/items, duplicate IDs, unowned equipment or slot mismatches
  cannot overwrite a valid primary or backup; validated backup recovery applies.
  Old activeWork records without quote retain their original deadline and rewards
  without inferring historical costs or applying the current outfit retroactively.
- This version is NOT an importer for catGameSaveV1 web JSON. Full web-save import
  requires a dedicated migration that preserves all unimplemented module blocks.

All UI messages are keys. ActionOutcome.amount means consumed units for care,
granted units for purchases, and awarded gold for work completion.
